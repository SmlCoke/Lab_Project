## Problem1

Condition 1: $n = 1.63, \lambda = 193\text{nm} , k_1 = 0.25$
Condition 2: $n = 1，\lambda = 157\text{nm}，k_1 = 0.25$

Thus,
Result 1: 
$$l_{\text{min}1} = \frac{k_1 \lambda}{NA \times n} \approx 31.83\text{nm}$$

Result 2: 
$$l_{\text{min}2} = \frac{k_1 \lambda}{NA} \approx 42.20\text{nm}$$

Thus, we have:
$$l_{\text{min}1} < l_{\text{min}2}$$

Thus, the immersion lithography mode at 193 nm wavelength outperforms the dry lithography mode at 157 nm wavelength.
